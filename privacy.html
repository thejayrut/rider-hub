Rider Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

What Rider Hub stores

Rider Hub stores motorcycle details, ride plans, gear lists, expenses, maintenance information, preferences and files that you choose to add.

Account data

When email account support is enabled, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, account identifier and authentication state. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored in Rider Hub browser code.

Cross-device data

Account state may be synchronized through Cloud Firestore under a user-specific account path. Security rules are designed so an authenticated user can access only their own Rider Hub records.

Private document files are not stored in Firebase Storage in the no-cost build. If you connect Google Drive, Rider Hub may store those files in your own Google Drive application-data area. Otherwise files remain on the current device.

Weather, maps and location

Rider Hub may request public weather data for ride locations and may open mapping services for routes. Location is requested only when you use features that need it, such as emergency location sharing.

Sharing and advertising

Rider Hub does not sell your personal data. Rider Hub does not intentionally expose private uploaded documents through the public GitHub repository.

Your control

You can log out, remove local browser data, disconnect Google Drive sync, or delete files you added. Account deletion support will be added with the public account backend.
